# Asmaa Studio Glossary

- Asmaa Studio: customer-facing brand for the photography and videography business.
- Asmaa Video: legacy account/domain wording still used by social handles and package PDF.
- Bride details: jewelry, bouquet, shoes, perfume, dress, and other detail shots.
- First Look: staged but emotional moment when the groom first sees the bride in her dress.
- Zaffa: wedding entrance procession video coverage.
- Kosha: wedding stage or seating backdrop area.
- BTS: behind-the-scenes clips from photo or video sessions.
- Booking policy: deposit, confirmation, cancellation, and remaining payment rules.
- Service area page: city-specific SEO page targeting Al Ahsa, Dammam, or Khobar search intent.
- Booking FAQ page: dedicated `/faq` conversion page answering repeated pre-booking questions in Arabic.
- WhatsApp conversion: the business goal of turning a visitor into a direct WhatsApp inquiry.
