# Asmaa Studio SEO Campaign

## Primary Domain Plan

- Canonical domain: `asmaa.video`
- Redirect domain: `asmaavideo.com`
- Sitemap: `/sitemap.xml`
- Robots: `/robots.txt`
- Conversion endpoint: WhatsApp link with source tracking text.

## Pages Created

- `/` main brand and packages page
- `/alahsa` local SEO page for Al Ahsa
- `/dammam` local SEO page for Dammam
- `/khobar` local SEO page for Khobar

## Next Actions After Deployment

1. Submit `https://asmaa.video/sitemap.xml` to Google Search Console.
2. Create or optimize Google Business Profile using the same phone number, service areas, and website URL.
3. Add the website URL to Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp Business profile, and all highlight descriptions.
4. Create local backlink/citation entries where appropriate: wedding directories, local business directories, and venue/vendor partner pages.
5. Publish weekly Arabic posts targeting one location and one use case, then link back to the matching service-area page.

## Measurement

- Track WhatsApp source text by page: `hero`, `package-01`, `alahsa`, `dammam`, `khobar`.
- Review weekly: impressions, clicks, WhatsApp conversations, booked dates, and booking source.
- Keep one canonical phone number and domain across all platforms.

## Research Notes

- DataReportal's Digital 2026 Saudi Arabia report shows Instagram and TikTok remain major discovery channels in Saudi Arabia, supporting the decision to keep social-first creative while adding search capture.
- Google Business Profile guidelines allow service-area businesses that travel to customers; if there is no staffed customer-facing office, the address should be hidden and service areas should be accurate.
- Google Search Central recommends LocalBusiness structured data with a fully qualified URL, telephone, image, and price range when relevant; the site includes LocalBusiness JSON-LD on the homepage.
